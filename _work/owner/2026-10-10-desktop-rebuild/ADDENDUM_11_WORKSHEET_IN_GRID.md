The adjustment worksheet must be built into the comparable grid itself, not opened from a separate screen and not set up by the appraiser. The app automatically places a small worksheet icon (a green ⊕ or calculator) on every adjustable row of the sales grid, the rental grid, and every per-unit line on 2–4 unit reports. The adjustable rows are GLA, site, age, bedrooms, baths, garage spaces, above-grade nonstandard and unfinished area, below-grade finished, nonstandard and unfinished area, basement, outdoor living, cooling, design and market exposure. The icon must reappear whenever the grid re-renders, and it must be added automatically when a new comp or unit is added. The appraiser can also open a worksheet by focusing the row and pressing Ctrl+J, or from a Worksheets tab in the ribbon that lists all of them.

Clicking the icon opens the adjustment panel right on that row, as a popover or docked side drawer, without leaving or covering the grid, so the subject and comp values stay visible. The panel asks for the rate per unit, the threshold and the rounding step. For each comp it shows a live preview of the subject value, comp value, difference, dollar adjustment, line percentage and adjusted price as the appraiser types. OK fills the adjustment for all comps at once, Cancel changes nothing, and Esc closes the panel. The math is (subject minus comp) times the rate. The result is zero when the difference is within the threshold, because the threshold gates the whole difference, and it is rounded to the chosen step. A half bath counts as half the bath rate, and a driveway or carport counts as zero garage spaces.

After applying, the icon shows a ticked state with the rate used, for example “$75/sf”, and hovering an adjustment cell shows its math. If a subject or comp value changes later, that row recalculates automatically with a brief highlight. A value the appraiser typed by hand is marked as manual and never overwritten. Net and gross percentages and adjusted prices update live. Ship two rate profiles, “Single family (18 North)” and “Multi-family”, using the defaults in adjust.py. Users can edit, create and switch profiles, the last profile used becomes the default, and an “Adjust all” button applies a whole profile in one click. On a signed report the panel opens read-only with OK disabled.
