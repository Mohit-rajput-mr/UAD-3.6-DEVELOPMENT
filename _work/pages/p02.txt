Client Instructions
 
******"NEW PUDS & UNITS IN NEW OR RECENTLY CONVERTED CONDO PROJECTS: Appraiser to provide 1
closed sale comp sale from inside the subject subdivision/same builder and 2 closed sale comps from
outside the subject subdivision and builder influence."
On all New Construction PUDs or Condos- a minimum of 3 closed comp sales are required. MUST HAVE,
no exceptions: 1 inside subdivision, subject builder 1 outside subdivision, different builder Also, need
1 more, MUST BE OUT OF BUILDER INFLUENCE 1 additional, outside builder influence. Can be inside or
outside the subdivision, as long as it is outside builder influence. This means a competing builder or
resale. *********
In conformance with Fannie Mae’s requirements around Gross Living Area Calculation, effective 04/01/2022, all
Appraisers must follow ANSI® Z765-2021 standards on measuring, calculating, and reporting the GLA and non-GLA
areas of the subject. Please refer to Fannie Mae Selling Guide section B4-1.3-05 for additional detail. The appraiser
must include 1 of the below statements in the appraisal: A statement that ANSI Z765-2021 measuring standards
were applied Declaration 1, 2 or 3 as outlined in the ANSI Z765-2021 For properties where the entire square
footage is below grade, such as a berm home, or the specific state requires the appraiser to adhere to a different
measuring standard and the appraiser is unable to adhere to the ANSI standard, the appraiser must enter
“GXX001-” at the beginning of the Additional Features field of the appraisal report and provide a detailed
explanation of why they were not able to comply
*If the appraiser is unable to provide the required comp(s), a detailed explanation is needed in the report.
*Preferred- to have the builder name listed in the comp GRID. This is not a requirement but would be very helpful to
confirm we have the requirements met and prevent back and forth with AMC/Appraiser for confirmation or revisions.
