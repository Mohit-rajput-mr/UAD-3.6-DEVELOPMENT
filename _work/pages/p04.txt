 o FNMA/HUD guidelines are to be followed on all FHA reports.
o The FHA Case # must be shown at the top of every page of the appraisal report PDF.
o An explanation of whether or not the subject conforms to HUD minimum standards is required.
o Appraiser must operate appliances that are part of the property that are being conveyed, e.g., turning on the
stove, operating the dishwasher, et cetera
o PHOTO REQUIREMENTS- Original photos are required. NO MLS PHOTOS o PHOTOS DEPICTING ALL 4 SIDES
of the subject property
o PHOTOS of the street in BOTH directions
 o PHOTOS of the ATTIC & CRAWL as applicable
 o Remaining Economic Life (REL) is required for all interior inspections including condos. A head and shoulders
inspection of the attic is required, if applicable. A comment specifically stating this must also be provided.
 o The mechanicals (heat, water, and electric) must be tested and in proper working order. A comment specifically
stating this must also be provided.
 o Explanations of deficiencies of the subject property are required, and must be addressed in the appropriate
section of the report.
o The sketch must include finished lower level/basement areas and their room locations, as well as all outbuildings,
patios, decks, et cetera - Porches & Decks, must indicate as ‘covered’ or ‘uncovered’
 o If the subject is legal non-conforming use according to its zoning, the appraiser must indicate whether or not the
improvements can be legally rebuilt if destroyed. If the subject meets FHA minimum standards the appraiser must
state that the above items were checked and the subject property meets HUD property requirements as per HUD
handbook 4000.1. Even with the above requirements, some lenders will still condition appraisals. -
