Public Remarks
Nestled in the picturesque Village of Roslyn Harbor and located within the highly acclaimed Roslyn School District, this beautifully maintained 10-
room Colonial offers the perfect blend of timeless elegance and modern comfort. 4 bedrooms and 2.5 baths, this exceptional residence combines
spacious living with thoughtful design.. A private, landscaped front yard creates a true hidden oasis, offering a sense of privacy and retreat. A full
brick driveway guides you toward the home, gracefully back from the street, revealing its character and charm as you make your way toward the
home. A welcoming covered front porch, balcony and refined brick-and-vinyl exterior sets the tone for the warmth and sophistication found
throughout the home. Inside, an open-concept layout is designed for seamless everyday living and effortless entertaining. The main level
showcases refined details including crown molding, a cozy decorative fireplace, a dedicated laundry area with washer and dryer, and a spacious
chef’s kitchen outfitted with luxury stainless-steel appliances—highlighted by a Thermador 48" range with custom hood and a Sub-Zero
refrigerator. Upstairs, the expansive primary suite offers a private retreat complete with a walk-in closet and a spa-inspired en-suite bath, and
seasonal winter water vires. Three additional generously sized bedrooms and a well-appointed full bathroom provide comfort and flexibility for
family or guests. The lower level expands the living space with a full basement offering abundant storage and recreation potential, featuring a
textured brick curved counter bar ideal for entertaining. Additional highlights include a detached two-car garage, a storage shed for added
convenience, and sliding glass doors that open to a beautifully finished paver patio and lush backyard—perfect for outdoor dining, gatherings, or
quiet relaxation in a serene setting. Ideally located in one of the North Shore’s most desirable communities, this exceptional residence combines
classic Colonial charm with thoughtful modern upgrades, offering spacious living, top-rated schools, and an unmatched lifestyle in Roslyn Harbor.
