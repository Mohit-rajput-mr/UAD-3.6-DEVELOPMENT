Public Remarks
Welcome to this sun-drenched residence nestled within the highly sought-after Roslyn Country Estates community. Move-in ready and filled with
natural light, this inviting home offers the perfect blend of comfort today and endless possibilities for tomorrow. A gracious entry foyer opens to
an expansive living room featuring soaring ceilings, rich oak flooring, and a charming wood-burning fireplace. The adjoining formal dining room is
framed by walls of windows that showcase the property’s landscaping and seamlessly brings the beauty of the outdoors inside. The main level
offers a spacious eat-in kitchen, powder room, three well-appointed bedrooms, a full hall bath, and a private primary suite. The lower level
provides exceptional additional space with a large recreation and entertainment room, outside entrance, abundant storage, and flexible areas
ideal for a home office, gym, playroom, or guest accommodations. Step outside to a lush backyard retreat designed for both entertaining and
relaxation. Multiple outdoor gathering spaces are surrounded by specimen plantings and mature landscaping, creating a private and serene
setting. Enjoy membership to the coveted East Hills Park, offering access to multiple swimming pools, tennis and basketball courts, playgrounds,
fitness facilities, walking trails, a dog park, and year-round activities for all ages. Gas service has already been installed and available for future
connection if desired. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy North Shore living in one of the area’s most desirable communities.
