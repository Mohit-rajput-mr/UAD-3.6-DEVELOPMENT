Public Remarks
Located in the highly desirable Roslyn School District in Greenvale, this beautifully updated home offers the perfect blend of comfort and
elegance. Built in 2004 and recently renovated in 2026, the property features approximately 4038 sq ft of interior space (see floor plan photos).
The main level boasts a spacious living room with a fireplace, a formal dining room, and an open-concept kitchen equipped with new appliances.
Additional highlights include central air conditioning with multiple zones, two half baths, sliding doors leading to the backyard, and a convenient
mudroom with laundry. his thoughtfully designed first floor is ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The second-floor features four
generously sized bedrooms and three full bathrooms, including a luxurious primary suite with a Jacuzzi tub. Finished basement with a separate
entrance and a lot of storages, a wide driveway that accommodates multiple vehicles. The fenced private backyard provides the perfect setting
for outdoor gatherings and relaxation. Conveniently located near Wheatley Plaza, Americana Manhasset, the Long Island Rail Road, and public
transportation, this home also offers easy access to the historic village of Roslyn, celebrated for its dining, shops, and charming character. Just a
short drive to the water, it combines the best of North Shore living with low taxes.
