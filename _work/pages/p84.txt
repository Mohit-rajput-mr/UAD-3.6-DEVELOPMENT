Public Remarks
Welcome to 14 Mackay Way, a beautifully maintained Colonial situated in Roslyn’s desirable North Shore community and within the highly
regarded Roslyn School District. This spacious 4-bedroom, 2.5-bath home offers approximately 2,806 sq. ft. of living space, combining a
traditional Colonial layout with bright, comfortable spaces for today’s lifestyle. The main level features a gracious formal living room and dining
room, along with a spacious open kitchen offering abundant cabinetry, granite countertops, double sinks, gas cooking, a center island and a sun-
filled breakfast area. The kitchen flows naturally into the inviting family room with fireplace, creating an ideal setting for everyday living and
entertaining. Upstairs are four generously sized bedrooms, including a spacious primary suite, along with beautifully appointed marble baths.
Hardwood floors, abundant natural light, two-zone heating and central air add to the home's comfort and appeal. A large finished full basement
provides wonderful additional recreational space and features a home theater setup, with plenty of room for entertainment, a playroom, gym,
home office or storage. Outside, the home offers attractive landscaping, an impressive circular driveway with ample parking, a convenient side
entrance and an attached two-car garage. Enjoy an exceptional Roslyn location just minutes from historic Roslyn Village, Roslyn Public Library,
the Clock Tower, shopping and popular dining destinations. Roslyn LIRR Station is approximately five minutes away, offering convenient access to
Manhattan. Nearby N20G/N20H bus service provides access toward Flushing, while Northern Boulevard, the LIE and Northern State Parkway are
all within easy reach. A wonderful opportunity to enjoy generous living space, convenience and the sought-after Roslyn lifestyle. A Must See.
